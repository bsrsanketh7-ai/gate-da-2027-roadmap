A normal study day

2.5 hThis week's main topic: learn it, then solve problems on it the same day.
1.5 hThe side track: Python and DSA in weeks 1–7, then practice questions.
30 minGeneral Aptitude: 5–10 questions every day.
30 minRevise yesterday's notes and update your formula sheet.
SundayTimed test on the week's topics, then redo every wrong answer. Weekends are 7–8 hours.

Free resources

  • Official DA papers 2024–26 and syllabus: GATE 2027 site
  • Linear algebra: MIT 18.06 (Gilbert Strang) and 3Blue1Brown
  • Probability: Harvard Stat 110 (Joe Blitzstein) lectures on YouTube
  • ML intuition: StatQuest; depth: NPTEL "Introduction to Machine Learning" (IIT Madras)
  • AI search, Bayes nets, sampling: Berkeley CS188
  • Extra practice: GATE CS past papers on probability, linear algebra, calculus, DSA and DBMS. Those topics are the same in both papers.

Rules that save marks

  • Keep an error log: every wrong question, why you got it wrong, and the fix.
  • Keep a single formula sheet and read it every Sunday.
  • Practise with the GATE virtual calculator, not a phone calculator.
  • For DA, math and ML questions are numeric. Practise getting exact answers under time pressure.
